About Hearth & Field

Who we are

Hearth & Field Ltd is a UK homeware retailer selling bedding, furniture, lighting, kitchenware, bathroom accessories, rugs, decor and mattresses. We were founded in Leeds in 2017.

Online only

We sell online only and do not have shops. Our warehouse is at Unit 4, Aire Valley Park, Leeds, LS9 0PT, and is not open to the public.
