Frequently Asked Questions

How much does delivery cost?

Standard delivery is £3.95 and takes 3 to 5 working days. It is free on orders over £50. Express next working day delivery is £6.95 if you order before 14:00 Monday to Thursday. Large furniture two-person delivery is £29 and takes 7 to 10 working days.

Do you ship internationally?

No. We deliver to the UK mainland, Northern Ireland, the Isle of Man and the Channel Islands only. We do not offer international shipping.

How long do I have to return something?

You can return unused items in their original packaging within 30 days from delivery using a free returns label from our Returns Portal.

When will I get my refund?

Refunds go back to your original payment method within 5 working days of your return reaching our Leeds warehouse.

Can I return an opened duvet or pillow?

For hygiene reasons, opened bedding, pillows and mattresses cannot be returned unless they are faulty.

Do you have a phone number?

We do not have a phone line. Please email support@hearthandfield.co.uk or chat with us in the Help Centre.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Visa, Mastercard, Amex, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay and Klarna. Klarna splits orders of £30 to £1,000 into 3 instalments. All prices include 20% VAT.
