Using the Returns Portal

Step 1: Start your return

Open the Returns Portal and enter your order number and the email address you ordered with.

Step 2: Choose items and a reason

Select the items you want to return and tell us why. Items must be unused and in their original packaging, within 30 days of delivery.

Step 3: Print your free label

We email you a free returns label. Attach it to the parcel and drop it at the carrier location shown. For two-person furniture, we arrange a free collection instead.

Step 4: Get your refund

Your refund is issued to your original payment method within 5 working days of your return reaching Leeds.
