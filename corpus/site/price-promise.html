Our Price Promise

How it works

If you find an identical item cheaper at another UK retailer within 7 days of buying from us, we will refund the difference.

What is excluded

The Price Promise does not apply to marketplaces such as eBay or Amazon Marketplace sellers, clearance or outlet prices, or items that are not identical.

How to claim

Email us your order number and a link to the cheaper item within 7 days of your purchase. We will check it and refund the difference to your original payment method.
