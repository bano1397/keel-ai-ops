Tracking Your Order

Finding your tracking link

When your order leaves our Leeds warehouse we email you a tracking link from the carrier, which will be Royal Mail, DPD or Evri. Most orders are dispatched within 1 to 2 working days.

Order statuses explained

Processing means we are preparing your order. Dispatched means it has left our warehouse. In transit means the carrier has it on its way to you. Delivered means the carrier has confirmed delivery.

Ask our assistant

Our Help Centre assistant can check the status of any order. Just give your order number, which starts with HF- followed by six digits, from the email address you used to order.

My order is late

If your order has not arrived within 3 working days of its estimated date, contact us and we will investigate with the carrier.
