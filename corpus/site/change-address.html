Changing Your Delivery Address

When you can change your address

You can change the delivery address while your order is still processing. Once it has been dispatched the carrier cannot redirect it, and you may need to arrange a return instead.

How to change it

Send us your order number and the full new address including postcode. Our assistant can update it straight away if the order has not yet been dispatched.

Addresses we can deliver to

The new address must be in an area we deliver to: the UK mainland, Northern Ireland, the Isle of Man or the Channel Islands.
