Cancelling an Order

Can I cancel my order?

You can cancel an order free of charge while its status is processing, before it has been dispatched. Ask our assistant or email us with your order number.

After dispatch

Once an order has been dispatched it can no longer be cancelled. Instead, you can return it within 30 days of delivery using our free Returns Portal.

Refunds for cancelled orders

Cancelled orders are refunded to your original payment method, usually within 5 working days. Klarna will update your payment schedule.
