Business & Trade Accounts

Who can apply

Interior designers, landlords, hotels, cafés and other businesses can apply for a Trade account.

Benefits

Trade customers receive 15% off and 30-day invoice terms.

How to apply

Email support@hearthandfield.co.uk with your company name, registration number and expected order volume. We aim to review applications within 3 working days.
