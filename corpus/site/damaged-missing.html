Damaged or Missing Items

Report it within 48 hours

If something arrives damaged, report it within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the item and its packaging.

Items under £40

Damaged items under £40 are replaced or refunded without you needing to send them back.

Missing fixings

If fixings or small parts are missing from flat-pack furniture, we send replacements free within 3 working days.

Missing parcels

If your tracking shows delivered but you cannot find your parcel, check with neighbours and any safe place, then contact us within 48 hours and we will open an investigation with the carrier.
