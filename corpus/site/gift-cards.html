Gift Cards

Buying a gift card

Our e-gift cards are available from £10 to £250 and are delivered by email.

Validity

Gift cards are valid for 24 months from purchase and can be spent over several orders.

Restrictions

Gift cards are not exchangeable for cash and cannot be used to buy other gift cards.

Checking your balance

Enter your gift card number at checkout or on this page to see your balance.
